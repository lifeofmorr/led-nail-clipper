FAQ

How long does delivery actually take?

Orders ship from a US Amazon fulfillment center. Most US orders arrive within 5-10 business days of ordering. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. If a delay pushes past that window, contact us and we'll tell you exactly where your order is.

Does the light guarantee I won't cut the quick?

No tool can guarantee that. The light improves visibility, but you should still trim only the tip, in small cuts, and stop if the nail cross-section starts to look pink or dark in the centre.

Which pets is it sized for?

Cats and kittens per the listing. Large, thick dog nails need a heavier-duty clipper or grinder.

What if my pet hates nail trims?

Do one paw — or one nail — per session, in good light, and stop while it is still going well. Frequency beats force.
